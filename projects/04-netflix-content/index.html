MACHINE LEARNING

Predicting Mature Content in Netflix Titles

Explored Netflix title characteristics and developed a classification workflow for mature-content prediction, including exploratory analysis, data preparation, model evaluation, and discussion of responsible use.

Dataset

Netflix title data with content attributes used to study mature-content patterns.

Methods

EDA, preprocessing, classification modeling, confusion-matrix and classification-metric evaluation.

Key Findings

The workflow demonstrates how metadata can support content classification while highlighting the limits of automated labeling.

Business Value

Use model output as decision support and preserve review for ambiguous titles.
